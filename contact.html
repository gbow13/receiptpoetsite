Prints

Our prints are made using theprintspace.co.uk and are available as high quality Silver-based Digital C-type prints on Fuji Matt paper. This is my first edition of prints and they're limited to an edition of 20. Each one is signed by the artist.

Commissions.

Personalise your receipt exactly how you want. I've established that every single word is able to be spelled out so the dictionary is your oyster. These could be an acrosstic poem of someone's name, any other word, or a reproduction of a receipt I've made in the past. Although these are subject to availability as some receipt I cannot be sure of being able to recreate. These start from £150 plus the cost of the items.
